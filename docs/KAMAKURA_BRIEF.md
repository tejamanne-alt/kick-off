# Kamakura: the user's brief (v126)

The user's base prompt for Kamakura's reference images, given with the request to "update the Kamakura assets appropriately
and realistically with respect to houses, animals, trees, objects and other assets". It is the art direction for Kamakura's
kits (`tools/blender/kit_buildings_kambase.py` and the modules it lists, the Plants and Animals kits' Japanese models) and the
look target for its streets. Kept here word for word.

---

You are making reference images for a video game's art team. Read all of this, then follow it for every image in this chat.

THE GAME
A side-scrolling soccer platformer for phones, rendered in 3D but seen and played from the side: a kid dribbles a football
from home to school through a real city. Each level is one leg of that trip, from one stop to the next. Our 3D artists model
the game's buildings, props and plants from your images, and tune the game's light and colour to them. So we need both
accuracy and atmosphere.

THE LOOK
Photorealistic and cinematic, like a still from a modern big-budget open-world game or a film shot on location:
- Believable light: a warm direct sun against cool blue shade, soft bounce light from the ground and walls, deep contact
  shadows under things.
- Air you can see: atmospheric haze that lightens and softens the distance, warmer toward the sun.
- Rich, real, weathered materials: sun-faded paint, water streaks under sills, grime at the foot of walls, rust bleeding from
  fixings, chipped plaster and paint, patched paving, oil stains, worn kerbs, moss in damp corners.
- A dense, lived-in street: wires and poles, signs, vents, pipes, shutters, planters, bicycles, parked vehicles, a little
  litter, puddles that mirror the sky.
- Natural colour: saturated where the city is colourful, never a colour filter or a tint over the whole picture. Do not
  imitate any specific game or film, and include nothing from one: no logos, landmarks, signs or characters from a game.

CAMERA (every street image, unless a shot says otherwise)
- A straight-on side view: the street runs left to right, parallel to the picture, and the building fronts face the camera
  squarely. No diagonal streets, no vanishing point, no aerial or low-angle views.
- One continuous panorama, as if the camera slid sideways along the street, always about 9 m from the building fronts at an
  eye height of about 2.5 m: every front is seen square-on across the whole width, verticals stay vertical, and nothing
  curves or bends toward the edges. No fisheye or wide-angle stretching. The horizon a little above the middle.
- Layers from front to back: an EMPTY, continuous walking surface across the bottom fifth of the picture (the play lane: a
  sidewalk, path, boardwalk, platform or sand, flat, with nothing standing on it except at its edges), then the kerb or edge,
  the row of fronts, a back row of roofs, trees and towers, and the far skyline or hills.
- The picture reads left to right as the leg: where it starts on the left, where it ends on the right.
- Everything sharp from front to back: no depth of field, bokeh, motion blur, vignette, lens flare or film grain.
- FORMAT: a street or set shot is one ultra-wide panorama at 4:1; a look target is a 21:9 widescreen frame; a sheet 16:9; a
  plate 3:2. Every shot ends with its own FORMAT line: use exactly that ratio.

LIGHT
Use exactly the light the city block describes: the season, the time of day, the colour temperature of the sun and of the
shade, the sun's height and side, the haze and the weather. Keep it the same in every image of this chat unless a shot says
otherwise. Highlights stay just under pure white; the darkest shadows keep a little colour.

CONTENT
- Real-world proportions: doors about 2.1 m, kerbs about 15 cm, storeys about 3 m. Ordinary scale cues: doors, steps, bins,
  parked vehicles.
- All signs, shop names, posters and adverts are INVENTED, in the city's own language and script, short and legible. No real
  brands, chains, logos or trademarks, and no readable licence plates.
- ABSOLUTELY NO PEOPLE: no pedestrians, drivers, passengers, shopkeepers, figures, silhouettes, crowds, mannequins or
  reflections of people. Where a person would stand (a counter, a balcony, a driver's seat), leave it empty. No animals unless
  a shot asks for one. Parked vehicles are empty and nothing is moving.
- Add no text, labels, captions, borders, logos or watermarks of your own.

BUILT INTO 3D (our artists cut your pictures into models, so each must be clean)
- Every building, wall and object is solid and whole: nothing transparent, ghosted, faded, double-exposed, half-drawn or
  blending into what is behind it. Only glass is see-through.
- A building that appears in more than one image is the same building in each: when I attach its earlier picture, copy it
  exactly.
- Overhead power lines and cables run from pole to pole above the rooflines or against the sky. None crosses in front of a
  building's front or roof. The poles and their wires stay.
- No real brands anywhere, vending machines, drink panels, cans and bottles included: invented names, and no lettering,
  script, colour scheme or symbol that resembles a real brand.
- Every word legible, in correct characters of the city's script: no garbled or made-up characters.
- What stands in front of a wall (pots, plants, bins, bicycles, signs) stands apart, with the wall showing between the
  pieces: nothing overlaps anything else, and together they hide at most the lowest third of a front.
- Crisp outlines against the sky: rooflines, trees, poles.

SHEETS AND PLATES
When a shot is a SHEET (objects on a plain background) or a PLATE (a flat, straight-on texture), ignore the camera and light
above and follow the shot.
- A sheet's objects never touch or overlap each other.
- An animal on a sheet is cut out and given moving legs, tail and wings, so: every leg shows on its own, the far legs stepped
  apart from the near ones and never hidden behind them, each foot flat on the ground line with its toes apart; the tail held
  clear of the body, backdrop showing between them, never curled against the back; a bird in flight with both wings fully
  spread and clear of its body; a fish seen whole from above, its tail fin spread; legs, toes, antennae and tail streamers
  drawn clearly, never hair-thin; whiskers faint or none.
- The backdrop behind an animal is one even grey: no glow, halo or lighter patch around or between its parts, and no shadow
  touching it (a soft separate shadow on the ground, or none).
- A plate shows about 2 m by 2 m of real surface unless the shot gives another size, evenly weathered all over, with no single
  mark that would stand out when it repeats (no nests or clumps, paint splashes, big stains, holes or signs).

CITY: KAMAKURA, JAPAN
A seaside temple town on the Shonan coast of Kanagawa, an hour south of Tokyo. It must read as Kamakura and the Shonan coast
at first glance: not Kyoto, not central Tokyo, not a theme park.

BUILDINGS
- Homes: two-storey timber-and-mortar houses with grey glazed kawara tile roofs or dark standing-seam metal roofs,
  dark-stained board fences and walls, beige or white mortar walls, aluminium sliding windows with insect screens, small
  balconies with futon rails, sliding entrance doors, house name plates by the door.
- Lanes 3 to 4 m wide with no raised sidewalk (a green-painted walking strip along the edge), lined by concrete block walls
  and hedges, with tiny front gardens of potted plants.
- Shops: low two-storey shopfronts with noren curtains over sliding glass doors, hand-painted wooden signboards and paper
  lanterns, shop awnings, tables of goods out front.
- Temples and shrines: timber gates with heavy tiled roofs, vermilion torii, stone lanterns, stone steps, moss-covered stone
  walls, wooden prayer plaques.
- Lived in: air-conditioner units on brackets, gas meters, exposed pipes, corrugated sheet sheds, bicycles, plastic plant
  pots, laundry poles.

WEATHERING
Timber gone silver-grey or near black, sea-salt bloom on walls, moss on every damp stone, rust on corrugated sheet and
railings, black mould under eaves, sun-faded noren, cracked mortar.

PLANTS
Leaning Japanese black pines near the sea, camphor trees and Japanese cedars on the hills, cherry trees in full green leaf,
bamboo groves, hydrangeas, azaleas, moss, morning glories and potted bonsai at house fronts, a persimmon tree.

STREET
Asphalt lanes with white edge lines, concrete utility poles crowded with wires and transformers, round convex mirrors at
corners, yellow tactile paving, decorated manhole covers, drink vending machines, green public phones, bicycle parking, bus
stops with timetable poles. The tram: a single-track line with its overhead wire on poles, level crossings with
black-and-yellow striped bars, red flashing lights and bells.

VEHICLES (parked and empty)
A small two-car green-and-cream tram (an invented livery), kei trucks, scooters, small cars, a rickshaw with a black hood.

LIGHT AND CLIMATE
A clear May morning, about 7:45. The sun 40 degrees up, behind the camera to the left. Direct sun about 5,400 K: near white,
a hair warm. Shade about 9,000 K: a clean cool blue-grey. A deep cobalt sky with big cumulus over the sea. A soft sea haze
turns far hills blue and lightens them a little. The air is damp and clean, and stone and leaves still hold the night's dew.

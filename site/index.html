Questions

How do I play?

The arrows at the bottom left run. KICK touches the ball: drag on it to aim a chip, then let go. JUMP jumps (tap again in the air for a double jump) and SLIDE slides under barriers. Settings has a left-handed layout.

What are the stickers for?

Every level hides one. Finish a chapter's set, or reach 5, 10, 15, 20 and all 23, to unlock balls, outfits, flags and trails that can't be bought. Tap a sticker in the album to see it up close, or a missing one for a hint.

The school bell is too quick.

Switch the bell timer off in Settings and take each level at your own pace.

How do I start again?

Settings > Reset progress clears everything on this device. Progress is kept only on your device, so deleting the game deletes it too.

Does the game collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking: see the privacy policy.

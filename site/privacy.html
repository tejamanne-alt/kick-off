Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Kick Off 2 ("the game") is made by Astradite Private Limited ("Astradite", "we"). This policy explains what the game does with information about you. The short version: the game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

What the game keeps, and where

Your progress and settings (levels completed, stars, coins, stickers, the items you own and wear, sound and control settings, your choice of player) are saved only on your device. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game deletes them, and "Reset progress" in Settings clears them.

Gameplay statistics (for example, which level was played and how long it took) are written to a file on your device only, to help us test the game. They are never uploaded.

Co-op over Bluetooth

Co-op lets up to four phones in the same room race each other. The phones talk to each other directly over Bluetooth Low Energy; nothing goes through the internet or to us. While you are in a Co-op lobby or race, the other phones in it receive: the name you typed for Co-op (or "Player 2" if you left it empty), a random four-character tag the game made for your install (so two players with the same name can be told apart), which kid you play, and where your kid is in the race. Phones nearby that are looking for a Co-op game can also see your lobby's short name while you host one. The name and the tag are kept only on your device; leaving the lobby ends the sharing. The game asks for Bluetooth permission ("nearby devices" on Android 12 and newer) only when you open Co-op.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email addresses or contacts. The only name the game knows is the one you may type for Co-op (above).
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location, camera, microphone or photo access. (On Android 11 and older, Android requires the Location permission for any app that scans for Bluetooth devices; the game asks for it only for Co-op, uses it only to find Co-op games nearby and never reads your location.)
  • No chat, no user-generated content and no links out of the game.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

Purchases

If the game offers an in-app purchase, the payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details. The game only learns whether the purchase was made, so it can unlock the content and restore it on request.

Children

The game is suitable for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If the game ever starts handling information differently, this policy will be updated first and the date above changed.

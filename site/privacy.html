Privacy policy

Last updated: PUBLISH_DATE

Kick Off 2 ("the game") is made by Astradite. This policy explains what the game does with information about you. The short version: the game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

What the game keeps, and where

Your progress and settings (levels completed, stars, coins, stickers, the items you own and wear, sound and control settings, your choice of player) are saved only on your device. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game deletes them, and "Reset progress" in Settings clears them.

Gameplay statistics (for example, which level was played and how long it took) are written to a file on your device only, to help us test the game. They are never uploaded.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no names, email addresses or contacts.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location, camera, microphone or photo access.
  • No chat, no user-generated content and no links out of the game.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

Purchases

If the game offers an in-app purchase, the payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details. The game only learns whether the purchase was made, so it can unlock the content and restore it on request.

Children

The game is suitable for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If the game ever starts handling information differently, this policy will be updated first and the date above changed.

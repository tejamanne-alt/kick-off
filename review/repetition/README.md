# Repeated assets across the levels: audit and fixes

What came round again and again, how often, and what was done (generated content v106; see `PROGRESS.md`,
"Fewer repeats across the levels"). The new models are on `new_models.jpg`.

| What | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| A chapter's background (tree bands, back rows), tree shade, birds, poles and lamps | Identical in every level of a chapter (seeds made of the chapter and the level's start, which is the same everywhere); Fountain Square and Playground had the same street | Each level salts its seeds with its own name; its pole line starts at its own place; it sees its own stretch of the distant hills |
| Things to slide under | 3 to 5 looks per chapter for 8 to 11 uses: the market's stall and conveyor 4 times each, the beach's boardwalk and surf rack 3 times each, the park's trunk and bough 3 times each | 3 new looks (washing line, market barrow, wobble bridge); 7 re-chosen; no look more than 3 times a chapter, most twice |
| Knee-high walls (home, market) | The same planter, squashed to fit, 6 times | Planter, wheelie bins, stacked fruit crates |
| Tall walls | Drinks crates (market), and 5 identical hedges in the park | Fruit crates or drinks crates; hedges or an old block wall |
| Low steps | The same grey stone, 7 times | Stone, a flower bed (home), pallets (market, site) |
| Boxes to knock over | The same wooden crate in 4 chapters (13 stacks) | A moving box (home), a plastic crate (site), the wooden crate elsewhere |
| Lifeguard towers | One identical tower every 30 to 45 m on every beach | Every 55 to 80 m, each painted its own way |
| Rocks at the water's edge | The same rock cluster at every spot | A cluster, a big lone rock (unused models) or a pair of boulders |
| Palms on the beach | One green | Four greens |
| Behind the market | Blocks of flats only, about 14 per level | Flats, now and then a tall shop or a long terrace |
| Every adult (passers-by, their friends, builders, people at the stops) | One body, recoloured | Six rigged passers-by (the jogger, Mom, the office worker, the courier, the shopkeeper, Grandad) take turns with the adult, each dressed from its own wardrobe by walker and level (`review/passersby/`); the builder still to come (`PEOPLE_PROMPTS.md`) |

Left as they are, on purpose: the game's hazards and helpers (dogs, gulls, springboards, puddles, tides, swings, the
checkpoint flag) look the same everywhere so a player knows them at a glance; the home street at the start of a route
is the same home; a stop is the same place at the end of one level and the start of the next.

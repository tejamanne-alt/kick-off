# KM-B06 — Hase Station platform (corrected reference set)

Reference: KM1-1 (Hase Lanes). Intended asset dimensions: 10.0 m long, 3.0 m deep, 3.2 m high.

Images:

1. `1_front_left.png` — front elevation, 0–5.5 m measured left to right from front.
2. `2_front_right.png` — front elevation, 4.5–10 m.
3. `3_right_side.png` — right end elevation, complete 3 m depth.
4. `4_back_left.png` — rear elevation, left 0–5.5 m measured while facing rear.
5. `5_back_right.png` — rear elevation, 4.5–10 m.
6. `6_top_left.png` — overhead plan, left 0–5.5 m, front toward image bottom.
7. `7_top_right.png` — overhead plan, 4.5–10 m.
8. `8_three_quarter.png` — full structure from front-left, slightly above.

Front, back and top pairs were each made from a **single full-view generated master**, with approximately 1 m of repeated geometry, avoiding independent generation drift across their join. The source photo reveals mostly the front; unseen rear and roof details are plausible reconstructions, not measured survey data. The separate end and three-quarter views are independently generated and must be checked against the orthographic views before using them as exact construction drawings.

Surface target: muted weathered steel canopy, slender steel posts, concrete plinth, silver tubular railings, station and timetable boards, and IC card reader. Flat mid-grey background. These are high-resolution visual references, **not metrically calibrated CAD projections**.

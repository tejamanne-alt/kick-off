KM2-3 set pieces

KM2-3 Grandma's Whitebait Shop's set pieces, from your photographic KM2-3. Your picture looks straight at the beach road's frontage, so they are built as KM1-3's shops were: boxes and roofs whose faces toward the street are your picture's own pixels, at 215 px to the metre (the shop's opening 2.1 m to its lintel, its glass counter 0.9 m).

Ten models: Grandma's shop with its noren, its glass counter standing out (the bento on its cloth on top) and its signboard; the neighbour's two houses and their board fence; the whitebait drying rack in its parts; the fish boxes and the hose reel; the crates; the low sea wall with its steps; the promenade's wall, dune and pines beyond it and the pines behind the houses (cut-outs); the no-parking and bus stop signs. The neighbour's pots, the utility pole and the scooter are the kit's own, standing where your picture has them. They share one 4096 x 2048 texture.

10 models107 checks passed23 known limits0 failed40 not applicable

Known limits

  • Seen from the street they are your picture. Their sides, backs and roofs take KM-P3's boards and tiles in their own colours, or the part's own colour (check 4).
  • Your picture's top edge cuts the shop's and the neighbour's roofs: above it, the roofs go on in KM-P3's tiles to a ridge of their own.
  • Behind the rack, the pots, the pole, the boxes and the scooter, the fronts are painted from what each one holds: the fence's and the shop wall's boards stretched down, the shop's inside carried across from right of the counter, the walls and the pavement from beside the scooter.
  • The promenade's wall, the dune and the pines are one flat cut-out behind the steps, and the pines behind the houses another.

Still to come

  • Grandma herself, the calico cat, the swallow and the gull come with the errand's build, after your approval of the layout board.
  • Not installed in the game yet: they go in with KM2-3's level.

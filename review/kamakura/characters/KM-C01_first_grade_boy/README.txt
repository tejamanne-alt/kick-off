KM-C01_first_grade_boy: the first-grade boy (KM-C01), a passer-by in KM1-1 (docs/KAMAKURA_BUILD.md, "Characters (KM-C)").

1_front.png, 2_back.png, 3_left_side.png, 4_right_side.png
    The four pictures made in Gemini (Mia's realism, one view a picture, A-pose); the left side faces left, the right
    side faces right. Uploaded to meshy.ai as its Image to 3D views.
KM-C01_first_grade_boy.fbx
    Meshy's rigged character (was *_character_meshyrig.fbx): the model the game imports with tools/blender/meshy_rigged.py.
    About 14,000 quads (28,000 triangles), its textures embedded. Meshy's humanoid rig: Hips, Spine02, Spine01, Spine
    (the chest), neck, Head, arms and legs.
KM-C01_first_grade_boy_animations.fbx
    The same model with Meshy's animations (was *_animated_unity_humanoid_60fps_All_animation.fbx). Kept, not used: the
    game animates its characters in code.
KM-C01_first_grade_boy_texture.png
    Its colour texture (also embedded in the FBX).
meshy_maps/
    Meshy's metallic, normal and roughness maps: not used (the game's toon shading takes the colour alone).

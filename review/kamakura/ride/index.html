KM1-2 ride set

KM1-2 Ride the Enoden's set, from your three photographic set pictures KM1-2a to c. The ride is a fixed side-on camera inside the tram, so its set is the car's far wall across the aisle, the town passing behind its windows and the three stations seen through the door.

Five models: the car (its far wall your KM1-2a at 370 px to the metre, 11.2 m from bench to bench, its door 1.85 m; the windows' plain grey let go so the town shows through them; the benches standing out from the wall; the floor and the ceiling); the town passing (your KM1-2b whole, its ends meeting so the ride can repeat it); Yuigahama's, Wadazuka's and Kamakura's platforms, each cut from KM1-2c between its grey gaps. One 4096 x 2048 texture, the pictures at half their size, 30 triangles in all.

Known limits

  • The car's wall is flat: its straps, poles, posters and route map are drawn on it, as your picture has them. Only the benches stand out.
  • The town and the platforms are flat cut-outs: the ride slides the town past the windows, and a platform stands outside the door at its stop.
  • The image tool's mark is painted out of each picture.

Still to come

  • The ride itself (M1: the card reader, the sway, STOP at your stop) and the grandmother boarding at Yuigahama come with its build, after your approval.
  • Not installed in the game yet.
